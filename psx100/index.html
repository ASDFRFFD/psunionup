How Campaign Monitoring & Tweet Tracking Operates

The PSX100 network utilizes a tri-layer operational verification model to ensure transparent, authentic digital advocacy:

1Public X Algorithm Search

Real-time query ingestion via public X algorithmic filters tracks hashtag frequency, viral reach, and official retweets publicly.

2Participant Self-Verification

After posting, verified members log their @handle and district via the live check-in console to instantly reflect in war room analytics.

3District Coordinator Audit

Administrators and district leads cross-reference registered applications to confirm that all amplified posts originate from authentic Panchayat Sahayaks.
